## A. HR and payroll (Belgium)

### Joint committees and sectoral agreements
- A **joint committee** (paritair comité, PC / commission paritaire, CP) is a sectoral body of employer and union representatives. Every employer falls under one based on its main activity. PC 200 is the large catch-all committee for white-collar employees. [Agoria](https://www.agoria.be/fr/services/expertise/hr-legal-social-dialogue/consultation-sectorielle-et-commissions-paritaires/pc-200/cp-200-indexation-des-salaires-de-221-au-1er-janvier-2026)
- Collective labour agreements (CAO, collectieve arbeidsovereenkomst / CCT, convention collective de travail) signed in the PC set minimum wage scales (barema's), indexation rules, bonuses and sometimes meal vouchers and flexi-job opt-outs. [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/flexi-jobs-vanaf-1-juli-2026-mogelijk-alle-sectoren-tenzij-er-een-opt-out-welke-sectoren-zijn-al)
- Payroll logic has to be **PC-aware**. The same gross pay can produce different indexation, bonuses or benefit eligibility depending on the PC. [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/werkgever/nieuws/index-2026-in-paritair-comite-200-gekend)

### Annual leave and holiday pay
- Legal minimum leave is **20 days** for a full-time 5-day week (24 for a 6-day week). It is earned in the **previous calendar year** (holiday reference year, vakantiedienstjaar / exercice de vacances) and pro-rated for part-timers and new hires. [L&E Global](https://leglobal.law/countries/belgium/employment-law/employment-law-overview-belgium/11-employee-benefits/)
- **White-collar** (bediende / employé) holiday pay is paid by the employer. Single holiday pay (enkel vakantiegeld) is the normal salary during the leave. **Double holiday pay** (dubbel vakantiegeld / double pécule) is **92% of one month's gross salary**, usually paid in May–June. [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_be/technical/tax/tax-alerts/2026/attention-double-vacation-pay-is-just-around-the-corner) / [Vandelanotte](https://www.vandelanotte.be/en/news/annual-holiday-pay-in-belgium-what-for-whom-and-how-much)
- **Blue-collar** (arbeider / ouvrier) holiday pay is paid in one lump sum by the **holiday fund (RJV/ONVA)** or a sectoral fund in May–June. It is about **15.38%** of the previous year's gross wages (calculated on 108%). [Vandelanotte](https://www.vandelanotte.be/en/news/annual-holiday-pay-in-belgium-what-for-whom-and-how-much)

### Social security, tax, declarations
- The **employee social security contribution is 13.07%** of gross pay, with no ceiling. [Boundless](https://boundlesshq.com/blog/running-payroll-in-belgium/)
- Employer contributions are roughly 25% basic plus extras, so ~27% for white-collar and higher for blue-collar. Since 1 Jul 2025 the basic employer contribution is waived on quarterly pay above EUR 85,000 per employee. ⚠️ Treat these percentages as approximate. [Boundless](https://boundlesshq.com/blog/belgium-social-security-contributions/) / [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/introduction-of-remuneration-cap-for-calculation-of-social-security-contributions)
- **Withholding tax** (bedrijfsvoorheffing / précompte professionnel) is an advance on personal income tax. The employer withholds it every month using the official FPS Finance scales. The 2026 scales were lowered, so net pay is slightly higher. ⚠️ Do not hard-code rates; they change yearly. [Boundless](https://boundlesshq.com/blog/running-payroll-in-belgium/)
- **Dimona** is the immediate declaration of each hire and each exit to the NSSO (RSZ/ONSS). **DmfA** is the quarterly declaration of pay and working time, and it feeds all social security institutions. [socialsecurity.be](https://www.socialsecurity.be/site_fr/employer/infos/employers_nsso/which-declarations.htm) / [Settling in Belgium](https://settlinginbelgium.be/en/do-business/declaring-employees-in-belgium)

### Sickness: guaranteed salary and sick notes
- **Guaranteed salary** (gewaarborgd loon / salaire garanti): white-collar employees get 100% for 30 calendar days. Blue-collar workers get 100% for days 1–7, 85.88% for days 8–14, then an employer top-up on the health-fund benefit for days 15–30. From day 31 the health insurance fund (ziekenfonds / mutualité) pays. ⚠️ This comes from a secondary source; check the details with a payroll expert. [Playroll](https://www.playroll.com/employee-benefits/belgium)
- **Since 1 Jan 2026** employees get **2** days per calendar year off sick without a medical certificate, down from 3. Employers with fewer than 50 staff can require a certificate from day 1 through the work rules. [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/belgium/belgium-employment-law-update-new-rules-for-employees-on-sick-leave-or-with-medical-conditions-as-fr)
- **Since 1 Jan 2026** the **relapse period** is 8 weeks instead of 14 days: no new guaranteed salary is due if the employee falls sick again within 8 weeks. There is also no guaranteed salary for sickness during a partial return to work (the old 20-week limit was dropped). [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/belgium/belgium-employment-law-update-new-rules-for-employees-on-sick-leave-or-with-medical-conditions-as-fr)
- **Since 1 Jan 2026** employers with **50 or more employees** pay a solidarity contribution of **30% of the health-fund sickness benefit in months 2–3** of incapacity, for employees aged 18–54. [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/en/our-insights/infoflashes/incapacity-work-and-reintegration-long-term-sick-employees-whats-new-1)

### Time credit and thematic leave
- **Time credit** (tijdskrediet / crédit-temps): full-time, 1/2 or 1/5 career reduction (4/5 work). End-of-career time credit needs age 60, 24 months' seniority and a career requirement that rises from 2026. Since 1 Jan 2026 the 1/5 option is also open to people on weekend work or a 4-day full-time week, if a CAO or written agreement allows it. [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/en/our-insights/infoflashes/time-credit-reform-1-january-2026-end-career-main-focus)
- Career-break allowances (uitkeringen) are no longer paid for end-of-career reductions before age 60 (from 1 Jul 2025). [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/en/our-insights/infoflashes/end-career-time-credit-no-more-career-break-allowances-age-60-1-july-2025)
- **Thematic leave** (thematische verloven / congés thématiques) covers parental leave, medical assistance leave and palliative leave. Parental leave is **4 months per child per parent**, can be taken full-time, 1/2, 1/5 or 1/10, and must be used before the child turns 12. Birth leave (geboorteverlof) is 20 days since 2023. ⚠️ Changes for births from 2026 (extra days) come from a secondary source only. [CM](https://www.cm.be/en/faq-pregnant-birth/maternity-leave-parental-leave-birth-leave-adoption-leave) / [pregnantinbelgium](https://www.pregnantinbelgium.com/types-of-leave)

### Meal vouchers and company cars
- **Meal vouchers** (maaltijdcheques / chèques-repas): the maximum value rose from EUR 8 to **EUR 10/day on 1 Jan 2026**. The maximum employer share is EUR 8.91 and the employee pays at least EUR 1.09. The increase is **neither mandatory nor automatic**: it needs a sectoral CAO, a company CAO or an individual contract amendment. [Claeys & Engels](https://www.claeysengels.be/en-gb/news-events/meal-vouchers-maximum-value-increased-eur-10-1-january-2026) / [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/en/our-insights/infoflashes/final-go-ahead-meal-vouchers-worth-10-eur-2026)
- **Company car benefit in kind** (voordeel alle aard, VAA / avantage de toute nature, ATN) = list price × age coefficient × 6/7 × CO2%. The CO2% is 5.5% ± 0.1% per g/km relative to the reference, with a minimum of 4% and a maximum of 18%; electric cars are at 4%. The 2026 reference emissions are 70 g/km for petrol and 58 g/km for diesel, and the 2026 minimum benefit is **EUR 1,690/year**. [BDO](https://www.bdo.be/en-gb/insights/fiches/2026/benefits-in-kind) / [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/en/our-insights/infoflashes/company-cars-reference-co2-emissions-2026-have-been-published)

### Indexation
- **Automatic wage indexation** links wages to the health index. Each PC sets the timing and method. **PC 200** indexes every **1 January**: **+2.21% on 1 Jan 2026**. [Agoria](https://www.agoria.be/fr/services/expertise/hr-legal-social-dialogue/consultation-sectorielle-et-commissions-paritaires/pc-200/cp-200-indexation-des-salaires-de-221-au-1er-janvier-2026)
- ⚠️ **Cent index (centenindex)**, voted 29 May 2026: during two periods (from June 2026 and from Jan 2028) indexation only applies in full up to **EUR 4,000 gross/month**. Above that, higher earners get a smaller, capped increase (the cap works through a 2% reference). Sectors with no 2026 indexation apply it in 2027, which probably includes the PC 200 Jan 2027 index. The exact calculation per PC is complex, so defer to a payroll expert. [BDO](https://www.bdo.be/en-gb/insights/news-alerts/2026/what-does-the-cap-on-wage-indexation-or-the-%E2%80%98cent-index%E2%80%99-mean) / [L&E Global](https://leglobal.law/2026/03/23/belgium-the-wage-indexation-cap-on-wages-above-4000-eur-per-month/)

### "Arizona" federal government reforms (De Wever government, 2025–2029)
- **Voluntary overtime**: 360 h/year for every employee (450 h in hospitality, PC 302). The first **240 h are gross = net** (no tax or social contributions) and carry no overtime premium. In force **retroactively from 1 Apr 2026**. Part-timers qualify only under conditions. [L&E Global](https://leglobal.law/2026/05/27/belgium-reform-of-voluntary-overtime-retroactive-entry-into-force-since-1-april-2026/) / [Securex](https://www.securex.be/en/lex4you/employer/news/voluntary-overtime-regulation-from-april-2026)
- **Flexi-jobs**: open to **all private and public sectors from 1 Jul 2026**, unless a sector opts out by CAO (law of 28 Jun 2026, published in the Belgian Official Gazette on 2 Jul 2026). Care jobs need the proper qualifications. The flexi-wage is capped at 150% of the sectoral minimum (EUR 21/h in hospitality). The tax-free ceiling is EUR 18,000 (reported as indexed to ~EUR 18,440). ⚠️ Some older sources say 1 Apr 2026; the published law says 1 Jul 2026. [Unisoc](https://www.unisoc.be/articles/nl/public/uitbreiding-flexi-jobs-naar-alle-sectoren-vanaf-1-juli-2026) / [Certifisc](https://www.certifisc.be/nl/posts/flexi-jobs-de-definitieve-regeling-vanaf-1-juli-2026)
- **Unemployment benefits limited in time**: full benefits are capped at **24 months** (depending on career) under the new rules from 1 Mar 2026. People are being excluded in waves: the first wave from 1 Jan 2026 (very long-term unemployed), another large wave from Jul 2026, with ~180k people expected to be affected in 2026. [CM](https://www.cm.be/en/impact-changes-unemployment-2026-and-right-reimbursement-and-benefits) / [Brussels Times](https://www.brusselstimes.com/1591507/belgium-agrees-on-timing-to-end-long-term-unemployment-benefits)
- **Pensions**: the pension law was **voted 28–29 May 2026** and published in the Belgian Official Gazette on 1 Jun 2026. The **bonus** can be built up from 1 Jan 2026. The **malus** applies to pensions starting from **1 Jan 2027** if the career condition is not met (35 years of at least 156 days, 7,020 days in total). The legal retirement age is 66 now and **67 from 2030**. Unions announced a challenge at the Constitutional Court. ⚠️ Details are politically contested. [VRT](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/03/09/pensioenhervorming-2026-overzicht-maatregelen/) / [DeWereldMorgen](https://www.dewereldmorgen.be/artikel/2026/05/29/pensioenhervorming-goedgekeurd-door-parlement-vakbonden-trekken-naar-grondwettelijk-hof)

## B. EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970

- **Transposition deadline: 7 June 2026** (Art. 34). [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng)
- ⚠️ **Belgium (federal, private sector): NOT transposed as of Sept 2026.** On 1 Jun 2026 Employment Minister David Clarinval (MR) asked the Commission for a 6-month extension. There is no adopted text; the file is being discussed in the National Labour Council (NAR/CNT) and employers' federation VBO/FEB opposes it. The realistic date is late 2026 or early 2027. [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/en/belgium-requests-additional-six-months-transpose-european-pay-transparency-directive) / [L&E Global](https://leglobal.law/2026/06/30/belgium-misses-the-deadline-for-transposing-the-pay-transparency-directive/) / [Trusaic](https://trusaic.com/resources/global-pay-transparency-center/belgium/)
- The public sector is partly transposed: the Wallonia-Brussels Federation by decree, and the Flemish public sector by decree in force 7 Jun 2026. [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/be/en/services/tax/blogs/decree-partially-transposes-eu-pay-transparency-directive-for-flemish-public-sector.html)
- There is **no EU-level delay**: on 22 May 2026 the Commission said the directive will not be part of any omnibus or "stop-the-clock" package. [Ius Laboris](https://iuslaboris.com/insights/eu-pay-transparency-no-delay/)
- **Pay gap reporting (Art. 9):** employers with 250+ staff report yearly and those with 150–249 every 3 years, both starting **7 Jun 2027** (on 2026 data). Those with 100–149 report every 3 years from **7 Jun 2031**. Member states may go further. [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng) / [Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/11/eu-pay-transparency-what-employers-need-to-know)
- **Joint pay assessment (Art. 10)** is triggered when the report shows a **gender pay gap of 5% or more** in any category of workers that the employer cannot justify with objective, gender-neutral criteria **and** does not fix within **6 months**. It is carried out with workers' representatives. [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng)
- **Before employment (Art. 5):** applicants must receive the starting pay or pay range **before the interview** (or otherwise before the offer and contract negotiation). Employers **may not ask about pay history**, and job titles and recruitment must be gender-neutral. [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng)
- **Right to information (Art. 7), all employers regardless of size:** a worker can request their own pay level and the average pay levels by sex for workers doing the same work or work of equal value. The employer must answer within **2 months**, remind staff of this right every year, and cannot ban employees from disclosing their pay. The criteria used for pay and pay progression must be accessible (Art. 6). [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng)
- The **burden of proof shifts to the employer** and workers are entitled to full compensation (Arts. 16 and 18). [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj/eng)

### Belgian fraud landscape
- **Phishing losses:** ~EUR 49M in 2024 and **EUR 93M in 2025**, with 11,000+ cases (+30% year on year). Banks detect, block or recover about 75% of phishing-related fraudulent transactions (2024 figure). [VRT](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/08/phishing-actieplan-banken-beenders-febelfin/) / [Febelfin 2024](https://febelfin.be/en/themes/fraud-security/numbers-and-trends/figures-2024-if-it-smells-phishy-it-probably-is)

- **Febelfin / Minister Beenders action plan (8 Jul 2026):** a default transfer limit of at most **EUR 5,000** by early 2027; at least a **4-hour wait** before a raised limit takes effect, with notifications; fraud cases resolved within **15 business days**; an option for "slow banking" instead of instant transfers; temporary holds on suspicious payments; a shared fraud-data platform between banks; and **itsme security upgrades** by early 2027. [VRT](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/08/phishing-actieplan-banken-beenders-febelfin/) / [Febelfin PDF](https://febelfin.be/media/pages/publicaties/2026/febelfin-actieplan-voor-de-aanpak-van-online-fraude/01f6dccf4f-1783584006/febelfin-actieplan-voor-de-aanpak-van-online-fraude.pdf)

  - **"Hi Mum/Dad" WhatsApp messages.** [Safeonweb](https://safeonweb.be/en/news/beware-phishing-emails-lure-you-fake-helpdesk)
  - **itsme scams:** SMS or email asking the victim to "update/reactivate" itsme or to approve an action they did not start. itsme never sends links by SMS. [Stop Arnaque](https://stop-arnaques.be/en/itsme-fraud) / [Safeonweb](https://safeonweb.be/en/news/fake-messages-appearing-come-itsme-are-making-rounds-again)
- **Card Stop: 078 170 170.** [Safeonweb](https://safeonweb.be/en/news/beware-phishing-emails-lure-you-fake-helpdesk)
- Febelfin launched **Fraudstop**, a central contact point for online fraud (brochure dated 11 Aug 2026). ⚠️ Only the title was seen; no details verified. [Febelfin](https://febelfin.be/en/publications/tag:Fraud%20&%20security)

### Payments regulation
- **Verification of Payee (VoP)**, also called the IBAN–name check (naamcontrole begunstigde), is required by Instant Payments Regulation **(EU) 2024/886**. It has been mandatory in the euro area since **9 Oct 2025** and covers both instant and regular SEPA credit transfers. The results are Match, Close match, No match or Not possible. It is free, and the payer decides whether to proceed. [Febelfin](https://febelfin.be/en/press-room/fraude-veiligheid/verification-of-the-beneficiary-s-name-banks-start-gradual-introduction) / [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_be/technical/financial-services/financial-services-alerts/eu-verification-of-payee-compliance-steps-for-psps)
- **PSD2 strong customer authentication (SCA)** requires 2 of 3 independent factors: knowledge (something you know), possession (something you have) and inherence (something you are). It is specified in the EBA regulatory technical standards and applies since Sept 2019. In Belgium this is typically the bank app, a card reader or itsme. [Ping Identity](https://www.pingidentity.com/en/resources/blog/post/the-scoop-on-strong-customer-authentication-sca.html)
- ⚠️ **PSD3 / Payment Services Regulation (PSR):** provisional political agreement on 27 Nov 2025, final compromise texts in Apr 2026, ECON committee approval on 5 May 2026. **Formal adoption and publication in the Official Journal were still expected in H2 2026, and I could not confirm them as of 28 Sept 2026.** Most PSR rules (including SCA changes and fraud liability, such as refunds for bank-impersonation fraud) would apply ~18–21 months after publication, realistically late 2027 or 2028. ## D. E-invoicing (Belgium)
- **Mandatory B2B structured e-invoicing since 1 Jan 2026** for Belgian VAT-registered businesses in domestic transactions, via **Peppol**. It does not apply to B2C. FPS Finance (FOD Financiën / SPF Finances) runs the programme. [einvoice.belgium.be](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/partner/fod-financien) / [Loyens & Loeff](https://www.loyensloeff.com/insights/news--events/news/e-invoicing-in-belgium-as-from-1-january-2026-key-provisions-of-the-long-awaited-royal-decree/)
- A **3-month tolerance period** ran during Q1 2026, ending 31 Mar 2026, for businesses that could show they were working towards compliance. Since 1 Apr 2026 the fines are **EUR 1,500 / 3,000 / 5,000** for a first, second and further breach. [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/07/01/mandatory-b2b-peppol-e-invoicing-live-2028-e-reporting-next-2/)
- **E-reporting is planned for 1 Jan 2028:** near-real-time VAT reporting of invoice data to the tax authority through a Peppol "5-corner" model. Intra-EU B2B follows ~2030. ⚠️ This is planned and could still change. [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/07/01/mandatory-b2b-peppol-e-invoicing-live-2028-e-reporting-next-2/) / [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_be/insights/tax/how-belgiums-2026-e-invoicing-mandate-will-reshape-your-business)
- **Peppol** is an open network for exchanging e-documents. In its "4-corner" model the sender and receiver each connect through a certified Access Point. [EC eInvoicing BE](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/spaces/DIGITAL/pages/467108877/eInvoicing+in+Belgium)
- **UBL** (Universal Business Language 2.1) is the XML syntax used for invoices. **EN 16931** is the EU semantic standard for e-invoices. [Fiskaly](https://www.fiskaly.com/blog/belgium-b2b-e-invoicing-mandate-2026-peppol)
- **Peppol BIS Billing 3.0** is the Peppol specification (a CIUS of EN 16931, in UBL) and is the default format in Belgium. [Fiskaly](https://www.fiskaly.com/blog/belgium-b2b-e-invoicing-mandate-2026-peppol)

## E. Cross-cutting: AI Act and GDPR
- **AI Act (EU) 2024/1689 timeline:**
  - Prohibited practices apply since **2 Feb 2025**.
  - General-purpose AI (GPAI) obligations apply since **2 Aug 2025**.
  - **Art. 50 transparency** (disclosing AI, labelling deepfakes) applies from **2 Aug 2026** and was not delayed.
  - [CSA](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-high-risk-deadline-omnibus-20260/)
- ⚠️ **AI Digital Omnibus** (reported as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744): Parliament approved it on 16 Jun 2026, the Council adopted it on 29 Jun, it was signed on 8 Jul, published in the Official Journal around 24 Jul and **entered into force on 27 Jul 2026**. **Annex III high-risk deadline: 2 Aug 2026 → 2 Dec 2027.** Annex I (AI embedded in products): → 2 Aug 2028. The AI literacy duty (Art. 4) was softened to "measures supporting" literacy. ⚠️ Check the regulation number and dates against EUR-Lex. [CSA](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-high-risk-deadline-omnibus-20260/) / [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)
- **Annex III high-risk examples relevant to the hackathon tracks:**
  - **Employment:** recruitment and selection, decisions on promotion or termination, task allocation, monitoring and evaluating workers. This is the SD Worx track.
  - **Creditworthiness and credit scoring of natural persons** (fraud detection is excluded), and **risk assessment and pricing for life and health insurance.** This is the KBC track.
  - Pitch framing: human-in-the-loop, logging and explainability, even though the obligations now apply from Dec 2027.
  - [Praxikon](https://www.praxikon.com/en/posts/digital-omnibus-high-risk-postponement-december-2027)
- **Belgian DPA** = **GBA** (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit) / **APD** (Autorité de protection des données). [GDPRhub](https://gdprhub.eu/index.php?title=APD%2FGBA_%28Belgium%29_-_149%2F2023)

It may only be used where a law allows it or with an authorisation (now granted by the Minister of the Interior). The DPA has sanctioned unnecessary use. In a demo: never show real numbers and use synthetic data. [Stibbe](https://www.stibbe.com/publications-and-insights/belgian-law-of-25-november-2018-on-various-provisions-relating-to-the) / [DataGuidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/jurisdictions/belgium)
- Health data such as sick notes is GDPR Art. 9 special-category data. Payroll and HR AI should minimise it and keep medical details away from the employer. ⚠️ This is a general GDPR principle, not checked against Belgian-specific guidance.
