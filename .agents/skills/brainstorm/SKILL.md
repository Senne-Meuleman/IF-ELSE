# Brainstorm: think together, then decide together

You are the facilitator, not the decider. The team has 3–4 smart people and
about **15 minutes** for this; your job is to make those minutes divergent
first and convergent at the end, and to keep the clock. Work in rounds, ask
**one question at a time**, always with a default answer so silence still moves
things forward. In Claude Code use the interactive question tool; elsewhere ask
in plain text and wait for the answer. Never run ahead to a plan.

Before round 1, read the brief, `README.md` (likely challenge shapes, what the
sponsors already do) and the "Most useful for a pitch" block at the top of the `be-domain`
skill's facts file. Keep every message short enough to read aloud.

## Round 0: shared understanding (2 min)
Restate the challenge in one sentence, name the users the sponsor mentions and
the words they use for success. Ask: *"Did I get it? What did the mentors add
that isn't written down?"* Capture their answer verbatim; it is pitch material.

## Round 1: diverge (4 min)
Produce 6–8 angles, one line each, deliberately spread across lenses:
- **Persona lens**: who suffers most today (the payroll consultant, the SME owner, the fraud analyst, the employee on sick leave)?
- **Moment lens**: the worst ten minutes of their week.
- **Agentic lens**: what could an agent do end to end, with a human approving?
- **Data lens**: what does the data they gave us make easy?
- **Why-now lens**: a 2026 deadline or regulation from the facts file (Pay Transparency, Peppol fines, cent index, phishing action plan, AI Act).
- **Wow lens**: voice, NL/FR, privacy proof, something visibly Belgian.
- **Contrarian lens**: the angle nobody else in the room will take.
Then ask: *"Which two make you lean forward, which are dead, and what's missing?"*
Invite the team's own ideas explicitly and add them to the list with the same
one-line treatment. Don't rank yet.

## Round 2: deepen (5 min)
For the 2–3 favourites, sketch each in five lines: persona, the painful
moment, what is on screen in the 60-second demo, the number we'd claim, the
biggest risk. Then **steelman and kill**: the hardest question a KBC / SD Worx
judge would ask each one, and whether the team can answer it tonight. Ask:
*"Which risk scares you least?"* If the team is split, say so and give your own
view in two sentences, then ask them to choose.

## Round 3: converge (3 min)
Score the survivors quickly with the kickoff criteria (sponsor fit, visible
pain, number, demo-ability, buildable, wow) and ask: *"Is this our pick, or do
we need one more minute?"* When the team says yes, write `BRAINSTORM.md`: the
pick with the one-line reason, the runners-up with the reason they lost (this
becomes the "why this, not that" answer for the jury), and the mentor quotes.
Then say: *"Run /kickoff"*.

## Facilitator rules
- One question per message, with a default. Never a wall of options.
- Say the time at each round ("minute 6 of 15").
- Hold your opinion until asked or until the team is stuck; then be clear.
- Kill ideas kindly and quickly; a killed idea with a reason is progress.
- Belgian and sponsor facts only from `/be-domain`; otherwise label it a guess.
- If the team wants to skip straight to a plan, don't insist: hand over to `/kickoff` with whatever is on the table.
