# Materials

KBC cannot share client data at a hackathon: no transactions, no balances, no
invoices, no client figures. Bring your own synthetic data.

What is here:
- `sample-invoice-peppol.xml`: one synthetic Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 invoice
  (UBL 2.1) between two fictional Belgian companies. Use it to see the shape of
  the data your prototype would work with. All names, numbers, IBANs and
  references in it are made up.

Everything else (accounts, payments, more invoices, obligations, the person you
build for) you create yourselves. Keep it synthetic and say so in the demo.
