# Materials: Inbox to action

> SYNTHETIC practice data written by team IF-ELSE. Every company, person,
> address, email address and IBAN here is invented. It is not an SD Worx dataset.
> IBANs have a valid Belgian format and checksum so validators accept them, but
> they are random numbers, not known accounts. No national register numbers are
> included on purpose. Salaries are illustrative, not checked against sector scales.

| File | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `clients.csv` | 6 contacts, 4 clients | The client companies and their authorised HR contacts (one row per contact). |
| `employees.csv` | 30 | Every employee of those 4 clients on our payroll: PC, contract type, hours, IBAN, address, gross monthly pay. |
| `inbox.json` | 15 messages | One week of a consultant's inbox (21–25 Sept 2026), NL/FR/EN, as received. |
| `actions.md` | 8 actions | The payroll sandbox API your agent may call. |

Email domains are fictional. `@sdworx.example` and `@mail.example` are placeholders.
